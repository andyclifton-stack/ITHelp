Creating a new Calendar

  1. Open Google calendar on your computer
  2. On the left, click the + next to "Other calendars" then click "Create new calendar"
  3. Add a name and description
  4. Click create calendar
  5. To share with other people, click on it on the left bar, then select "Share with specific people"
