Recording your Google Classroom or Google Meet

  1. Open 'Screen Recorder' extension
  2. Capture 'Only Screen'. You do not need to capture camera as the Google meet already has your camera showing.
  3. Select 'System + mic' for the audio recording.
  4. Click 'Start Recording' and then click 'Chrome Tab and select your Google meet tab. Make sure the 'Share Tab Audio' box is ticked. (This will now record just that tab, even if you change tab)
  5. Carry on presenting/teaching as normal
  6. When finished, open the Screen Recorder again and click 'Stop'
  7. Optional: Watch the recording playback
  8. Click Save (It will automatically save to your downloads)

IF THE VIDEO IS NOT LOADING THEN PLEASE CLICK THE TOP RIGHT OF IT AND IT WILL OPEN IN A SEPARATE WINDOW
