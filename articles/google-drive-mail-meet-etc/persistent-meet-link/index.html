Persistent Meet link in Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar.
  2. Click on the date you wish to create the Event.
  3. Add a title and click on the date to add a date/time.
  4. Click where it says 'Doesn't Repeat' and select which ever is appropriate.
  5. Add a Google Meet by clicking the blue button.
  6. Copy the Google Meet info (button on the left of the settings cog) .
  7. Paste the information in an email to send to recipients.
