Extracting Data

iSAMS

iSAMS is extremely easy and very useful for pulling off data, follow the video or the steps below to get data from iSAMS into a spreadsheet.

Extracting Student Data

  1. Open up iSAMS
  2. Navigate to Student Manager
  3. Enter search criteria (if necessary)
  4. Tick students who's data you want to extract
  5. Select the pink drop down on the right hand side and click "Export Wizard"
  6. Select create a custom report (existing ones can also be used)
  7. Click next
  8. Select any fields of data you wish to extract and click next
  9. Click next again
  10. Check the preview list of what data will be extracted and press next
  11. Select .xlsx or .csv file depending on your need and press next
  12. Make sure you click on "Click here to download the file"
  13. Open your file in Google sheets, office 365 or Microsoft excel.
