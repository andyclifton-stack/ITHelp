Creating a Group

  1. Open the Student Manager module.
  2. Select the Custom Group Manager tab and click Custom Pupil Groups:
  3. Click Create a New Group.
    The Selection Management window is displayed:
  4. Enter the custom student group details
  5. Click OK to add pupils to the group.
  6. Use the filters to find the students that you want to select. Hold Ctrl to make multiple selections in the filter boxes.
  7. Use the checkboxes to select students.
  8. Click Save & Add More to view your selection.
  9. Click OK to confirm your selection.
    Your selected students are displayed on the right.
  10. Once your custom group is complete, click Save & Close.
  11. The custom group is listed in the Custom Pupil Groups tree
  12. Shared groups and private groups are listed separately in the tree. Click on a group to view more details.
