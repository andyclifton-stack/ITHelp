Signing in to iSAMS (web)

  1. Go to isp.onelogin.com
  2. Select ISP: Everything
  3. Click on iSAMS

If you are unable to login to iSAMS please send an email to support@claremontschool.co.uk

Sig ning in to iSAMS ( Mobile App )

  1. Download the iTeacher App from the Playstore / Appstore
  2. Open the app
  3. Type in the school code CLMT
  4. Click login with Onelogin
  5. Use your onelogin details
