iSAMS

Signing in to iSAMS (web)

  1. Open ClassLink.
  2. Sign in with your school account if prompted.
  3. Click on iSAMS.

If you are unable to login to iSAMS please send an email to support@claremontschool.co.uk

Signing in to iSAMS (mobile app)

  1. Download the iTeacher App from the Play Store / App Store
  2. Open the app
  3. Type in the school code CLMT
  4. Tap the school account sign-in option
  5. Sign in with your school account
