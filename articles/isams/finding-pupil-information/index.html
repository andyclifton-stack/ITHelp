Finding Information about Pupils

  1. Go to Student Manager
  2. Select the pupil and click on Student Profile on the RHS
  3. For Previous Reports click on assessment and reports
  4. For Rewards and Conduct info click ont Rewards and Conduct
    You can play on this bit and get any information that you need
