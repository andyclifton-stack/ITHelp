  3. Log in with your school email address.

Error logging in?

If you are already logged into a microsoft account (personal or other) it may try to sign you in to classlink automatically with no option to change account. To solve this follow the steps below

  1. Go to https://myaccount.microsoft.com/
  2. Select your profile picture (or initials) in the top-right corner
    If you do not see this then just click "sign in"
  3. Either sign out of your personal account or add another account
  4. Sign in with your school details
  5. Now your school account is signed in, go back to Classlink – it should now take you straight in via your school account, or ask you which account to use
