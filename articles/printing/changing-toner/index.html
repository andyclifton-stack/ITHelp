Changing Toner

Printing

Toner will be found either already placed by the correct printer, or in the main office.

Changing the toner in a printer

  1. Check the prints model number - Located on the front
  2. Click the "check status" button on the printer
  3. Click "check" next to "printer"
  4. Get the correct colour toner and make sure it is for the correct printer model
  5. Follow onscreen video
