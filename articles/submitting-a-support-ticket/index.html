Requests & Support

How to submit a support ticket

  1. Navigate to https://launchpad.classlink.com/ispschools
  2. Click on the Service Desk icon
  3. Click on Technology Service Desk
  4. Click on either Incident Request or Service request (whichever fits your needs best)
  5. Complete the details and log the issue or request.

Quick Solutions (without submitting a ticket)

Self-Help - Click on Self-Help to manually look through or search the knowledgebase for solutions.

Need more help?

For faults or requests, submit a ticket through the Service Desk so the IT team can track and respond properly.
