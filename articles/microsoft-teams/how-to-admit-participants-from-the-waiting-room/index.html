How to Admit Participants from the Waiting Room

Microsoft Teams

Admitting Participants from the Waiting Room

  1. Find your meeting in the calendar and click join to start the meeting.
  2. A notification will pop up when someone is waiting to join
  3. Click Admit on the notification to let the person in
    If there are multiple people waiting you can click Admit All
