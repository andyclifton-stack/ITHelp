Creating Microsoft Teams

  1. Sign in to the Microsoft Teams application with your school email address
  2. Open Office 365 (if you are using the Teams application skip this) and click on Teams on the left side bar
  3. Click on 'Teams' on the left bar again if you are not automatically put on the Teams page
  4. Click 'Create Team' (if you already have a team the create button will be in the top right)
  5. Select a Team type
  6. Add a name and description to your team, then click next
  7. Add/invite people to your team. e.g. Class teams will have a students & teachers option
  8. Click next/skip
