Sharing a Recording

  1. Log into Teams
  2. Go to the Chat tab
  3. Select the meeting chat
  4. Click on the recording to open it
  5. Click "Open in Stream" in the top right
  6. Click "Share" also in the top right
  7. Click Share
  8. Click settings cog next to "Copy Link"
  9. Change to appropriate setting for your scenario
  10. Click Apply
  11. Copy link and send in email or message.
